# Contributor privacy notice

Version 1.0

This notice explains how contribution records, agreement acceptance and public credits are handled.

## Information used

Xavier Larrea, based in Ecuador, is responsible for the project's contribution records. To review contributions, record agreement acceptance and provide credit, the project uses:

- Your GitHub account ID and username.
- Your PR, contribution snapshot, relevant authorship disclosures and review history.
- The agreement version, exact acceptance declaration, authenticated event and timestamp.
- Your chosen public credit name, optional profile link and contributed languages.
- Additional authority evidence only when needed to resolve who can grant the required rights.

Routine acceptance does not require identity documents or a separate signing service. Do not put legal names, signatures, addresses or private contact details in a public PR. If private evidence is needed, request a private contact route without including the evidence in that request.

## Public and protected records

PR content, public account information and selected credits are visible according to the repository's visibility and GitHub's own terms. Public checks may show the agreement version, covered revision and acceptance status. Public contributions can be copied by others and cannot reliably be recalled from every copy.

The project retains a protected copy of acceptance evidence so later edits to a PR do not erase the record. Access is limited to Xavier Larrea and people or service providers who need it for repository operation, legal advice, rights verification or legal obligations. Records may be disclosed where legally required or transferred with the project subject to applicable data protection requirements.

GitHub hosts repository activity under its own privacy terms.

## Purpose, retention and requests

Records support contribution review, performance and evidence of the contributor agreement, public credit and protection of the parties' legal rights. Private evidence is not used for advertising. Retain only the information needed for those purposes.

Acceptance evidence is kept while contributions are used and afterwards only as needed to establish or defend rights or meet applicable legal obligations. Records should be reviewed and removed when they are no longer needed. Removing public credit does not necessarily require removing evidence of the agreement.

To request access, correction, removal of public credit or exercise other applicable data rights, open a repository issue asking for a private contact route. Include no sensitive information in that issue. Applicable legal rights and identity verification requirements govern the response. GitHub and independent copies are subject to their own processes.
